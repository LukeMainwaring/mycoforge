# Garry Tan

President and CEO of Y Combinator (background knowledge, not from the source),
and author of **gbrain**, an MIT-licensed agent memory layer
(`github.com/garrytan/gbrain`, about 31k stars and dozens of commits a day at
capture). The KB holds one gbrain document so far, the [[Belief Ledger]]
design ([[2026-10-15 gbrain Belief Ledger Design (Garry Tan)]]), written with
contributors across 33 commits over two days and then annotated with
"as built" sections recording where the shipped waves deviated from it. The
design notes which open questions "Garry accepted" and which plan corrections
"Garry updates," so the doc doubles as a decision log.

gbrain is the implementation side of what the YC partners on this KB's other
pages describe: [[Tom Blomfield]]'s [[Company Brain]] and [[Diana Hu]]'s
legible company, built as a database with trust tiers, purge semantics and a
belief ledger rather than as markdown. It is on the `/scout` watch list in
`kb.toml` as a repo whose memory layer overlaps this KB's wiki layer.
