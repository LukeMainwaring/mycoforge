# Alex Lieberman

Founder of 10X Labs, "a company that helps transform existing companies into
AI native companies"; before that a co-founder of Morning Brew. Author of the
"30 features of an AI native company" X post that
[[2026-09-06 The AI Daily Brief - How to Build an AI-Native Company Today]]
walks through item by item; the list is tabulated in [[AI-Native Company]].
Cited secondhand: the host ([[Nathaniel Whittemore]]) reads and paraphrases
each feature, so wording is the host's except where quoted. The post itself is
not in `raw/`; capturing it would be the upstream reference. He sells the
transformation he describes, so the features are a consultant's prescription
and are held at medium like the VC and vendor sources in the cluster. His
recurring emphasis is cost: five of the thirty features are versions of
[[Token Efficiency]].
