# Kareem Amin

Co-founder and CEO of Clay, the most-quoted founder in
[[2026-08-20 The Claude Code Guide For Startups]]. Three positions: hire
tinkerers because "every role is becoming an engineering role"
([[Everyone Ships]]); "you build it and then you build it again… the fourth
time you build it, you get it right" ([[Build for Rebuilding]]); and the moat
is being self-improving, Clay as "a self-learning revenue engine"
([[Self-Improving AI Loop]]). Clay also automated bug triage end to end and
runs quarterly prototype reviews. All quotes are via the Anthropic guide; no
primary source in `raw/`.
