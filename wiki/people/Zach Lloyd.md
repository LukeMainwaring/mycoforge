# Zach Lloyd

Of Warp, whose product, Warp Factories, is open infrastructure for cloud
software factories. Interviewed on Latent Space
([[2026-11-03 Latent Space - The 2026 Software Factory Infra Stack - Zach Lloyd]]),
where he states three positions: a factory is a declared, measured artifact
defined in code, one per product surface, sitting on a stack from the
declaration up to the access points ([[AI Software Factory]]); the factory
improves itself when agents score stored runs and open PRs against its
definition ([[Self-Improving AI Loop]]); and measurement took Warp's own
cost per PR from about $80 to $15–20 ([[Token Efficiency]]). He calls the
emerging discipline factory engineering. All of it comes from a vendor
describing its own product.
