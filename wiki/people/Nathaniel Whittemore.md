# Nathaniel Whittemore

Host of The AI Daily Brief. The transcript never names the host, so the
attribution rests on the show's public byline; held at medium for that reason.
His commentary in
[[2026-09-06 The AI Daily Brief - How to Build an AI-Native Company Today]]
is where the KB gets its dissents from [[Alex Lieberman]]'s list: process maps
are context for redesign, not templates for agents
([[Organizational Legibility]]); a mesh of sources of truth rather than one
layer ([[Company Brain]]); cost per successful task needs a whole model
architecture, not just a router ([[Token Efficiency]]); mid-process human
intervention points are still unknown ([[Tiered Autonomy]]); and "everyone
manages agents" is too broad while "everyone can build" is safe
([[Everyone Ships]]). His own additions: a loop needs an objective success
metric ([[Self-Improving AI Loop]]), "everyone is now in the eval business"
([[Verification Bottleneck]]), and the feature the list is missing, clear
ownership, is "nothing short of a new management discipline"
([[AI-Native Role Archetypes]]).
