# Tu Vu

Google Research and Virginia Tech; corresponding author of WikiSkill
([[2026-08-27 WikiSkill - Compiling Agent Experience into Persistent Knowledge for Skill Evolution]]),
the paper behind [[Skill Evolution]]. Also a co-author of EvoSkill (Alzubi et
al., 2026), one of the three baselines WikiSkill beats, and of SealQA, one of
its five benchmarks, so the comparison is partly a group iterating on its own
prior method. Worth knowing when weighing the paper's claims; it also means
the baseline was run by people who know it. Lead author is Liyan Tang; the
other authors are Cyrus Rashtchian, Chun-Sung Ferng, Andrew Tomkins and
Da-Cheng Juan. The paper cites [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s gist as its starting
point.
